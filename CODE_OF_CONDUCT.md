# Code Of Conduct

MedEval welcomes thoughtful, respectful contributions from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about technical issues.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Avoid sharing private data, secrets, or sensitive records.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Submitting secrets, patient data, or private student data
- Misrepresenting synthetic demo outputs as validated benchmark results

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate
this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from
project spaces.
