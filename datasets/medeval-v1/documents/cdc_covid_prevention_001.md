# How to Protect Yourself and Others

Source: CDC, "How to Protect Yourself and Others." This MedEval seed document
is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development.

CDC frames COVID-19 prevention within broader respiratory virus guidance. Core
prevention strategies include staying up to date with COVID-19 vaccines,
practicing good hygiene, improving indoor air, and taking precautions when sick.
The page also describes optional additional strategies such as masking,
distance, and testing to guide next steps.

The source notes that people with risk factors for severe illness should seek
health care promptly for testing and possible treatment, because treatment may
need to begin within a few days of symptom onset.
