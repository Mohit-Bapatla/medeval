# Safe Use of Over-the-Counter Pain Relievers and Fever Reducers

Source: FDA, "Safe Use of Over-the-Counter Pain Relievers and Fever Reducers."
This MedEval seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation
development, not medication advice.

FDA advises consumers to follow directions when using common over-the-counter
pain relievers and fever reducers. The page says active ingredients in these
medicines are safe and effective when labeling directions or advice from a
healthcare professional is followed.

FDA warns that using more than recommended can cause serious injury. The page
links consumers to more information about nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs,
also called NSAIDs, and acetaminophen.
