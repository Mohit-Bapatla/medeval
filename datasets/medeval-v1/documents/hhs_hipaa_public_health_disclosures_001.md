# HIPAA Public Health Disclosures

Source: HHS Office for Civil Rights, HIPAA public health disclosures page. This
MedEval seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation
development, not legal advice.

HHS explains that the HIPAA Privacy Rule recognizes a legitimate need for public
health authorities and others responsible for public health and safety to access
protected health information for public health missions. The page says covered
entities may disclose protected health information without authorization for
specified public health purposes when the recipient is legally authorized to
receive the information.

Examples described by HHS include reporting disease or injury, reporting vital
events such as births or deaths, and conducting public health surveillance,
investigations, or interventions. The page also identifies public health
authorities as agencies or authorities responsible for public health matters as
part of an official mandate.
