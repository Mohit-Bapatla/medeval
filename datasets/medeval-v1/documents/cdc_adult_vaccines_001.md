# What Vaccines Are Recommended for You

Source: CDC, "What Vaccines Are Recommended for You." This MedEval seed
document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development.

CDC explains that adults should stay up to date on routine vaccines. The page
highlights COVID-19 vaccine, flu vaccine, and Tdap or Td vaccine as routine
adult vaccines. It also explains that other vaccine recommendations can depend
on age, pregnancy, health conditions, life events, job, or travel.

For pregnancy, the CDC page notes whooping cough vaccination during each
pregnancy and points readers toward timing and vaccine-specific information.
The page is appropriate for general schedule-grounded QA, but final vaccine
decisions may depend on individual history and current clinical guidance.
