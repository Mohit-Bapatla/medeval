# How to Prevent Norovirus

Source: CDC, "How to Prevent Norovirus." This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical advice.

CDC says norovirus is very contagious, but people can take steps to stop it from
spreading. Key prevention steps include washing hands well with soap and water,
not preparing or handling food when sick, and not caring for others when sick.

CDC says hand sanitizer alone does not work well against norovirus. The page
says hand sanitizer can be used in addition to handwashing, but it is not a
substitute for handwashing.

The source says norovirus can be found in vomit or feces before a person starts
feeling sick. It also says the virus can stay in feces for two weeks or more
after a person feels better.

CDC advises sick people not to prepare food, handle food, or care for others
until at least two days, or 48 hours, after symptoms stop. The source highlights
this as especially important for restaurants, schools, daycare, long-term care
facilities, and other places where people may expose others to norovirus.
