# Asthma

Source: NIH/NLM MedlinePlus, "Asthma." This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical advice.

MedlinePlus presents asthma as a chronic lung disease involving the airways
that carry air in and out of the lungs. During asthma symptoms, airways may
become inflamed and narrowed, which can cause wheezing, coughing, chest
tightness, and breathing difficulty. A period when symptoms get worse than
usual may be called an asthma attack or flare-up.

This source is useful for patient-education QA about basic symptoms and general
condition explanation. It should not be used to determine severity, choose an
inhaler, or assess an emergency.

## Evaluation Notes

- Good future question types: symptom identification, condition definition, and
