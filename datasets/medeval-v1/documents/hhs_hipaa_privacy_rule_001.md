# The HIPAA Privacy Rule

Source: HHS Office for Civil Rights, "The HIPAA Privacy Rule." This MedEval
seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not
legal advice.

HHS explains that the HIPAA Privacy Rule establishes national standards for
protecting medical records and other individually identifiable health
information. The page states that the rule applies to health plans, health care
clearinghouses, and certain health care providers that conduct specified
electronic transactions.

The page also describes the Privacy Rule as setting safeguards and limits on
uses and disclosures of protected health information. It gives individuals
rights over protected health information, including rights to inspect and obtain
copies of records and request corrections.
