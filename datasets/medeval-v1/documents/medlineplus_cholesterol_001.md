# Cholesterol

Source: NIH/NLM MedlinePlus, "Cholesterol." This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical advice.

MedlinePlus describes cholesterol as a waxy, fat-like substance found in all the
cells in the body. The source says the body needs some cholesterol to make
hormones, vitamin D, and substances that help digest foods.

The page says the body makes all the cholesterol it needs. It also says
cholesterol is found in foods from animal sources, such as egg yolks, meat, and
cheese.

MedlinePlus says HDL stands for high-density lipoprotein and is sometimes
called good cholesterol because it helps the body get rid of cholesterol. HDL
carries cholesterol from other parts of the body back to the liver, and the
liver removes cholesterol from the body.

MedlinePlus says LDL stands for low-density lipoprotein and is sometimes called
bad cholesterol because a high LDL level leads to the buildup of plaque in the
arteries.
