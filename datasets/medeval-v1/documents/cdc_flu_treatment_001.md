# Treatment of Flu

Source: CDC, "Treatment of Flu." This MedEval seed document is a concise
attribution summary for evaluation development, not treatment advice.

CDC states that flu antiviral drugs may be a treatment option for people who
get sick with influenza. Antivirals are prescription medicines and are different
from antibiotics, which do not work against influenza viruses. CDC guidance
emphasizes that antiviral treatment works best when started early, ideally
within two days after symptoms begin.

The source notes that many people with flu have mild illness and may not need
medical care or antiviral drugs. It also says people who are very sick or at
higher risk for serious flu complications should contact a health care provider
right away.
