# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04431453 Study of Remdesivir in Participants Below 18 Years Old With COVID-19

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT04431453. This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not clinical trial
enrollment advice.

The ClinicalTrials.gov record identifies the study as a completed
interventional Phase 2/Phase 3 study of remdesivir in participants below 18
years old with COVID-19.

The brief summary says the study goals were to learn more about remdesivir and
how safe it is in participants less than 18 years old with coronavirus disease
2019, or COVID-19.

Eligibility information includes participants under 18 years old with
cohort-specific age, gestational age, and weight criteria. Examples include a
cohort for ages 12 to under 18 years with screening weight at least 40 kg, and
cohorts for younger children, infants, and neonates with lower weight ranges.

The record lists severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 infection
confirmed by polymerase chain reaction as a key inclusion criterion.
