# ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00004992 Diabetes Prevention Program

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov study record NCT00004992. This MedEval seed
document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development.

The study record identifies the Diabetes Prevention Program as a completed
Phase 3 interventional study sponsored by the National Institute of Diabetes
and Digestive and Kidney Diseases. The brief summary describes a nationwide
clinical study asking whether type 2 diabetes can be prevented or delayed in
volunteers at high risk for developing diabetes.

The study record lists randomized parallel arms including placebo, metformin,
and intensive lifestyle intervention. Eligibility information includes impaired
glucose tolerance, a minimum age of 25 years, all sexes, and body mass index
criteria. Exclusion criteria include underlying disease likely to limit life
span or increase intervention risk, diabetes or disordered glucose metabolism,
suboptimally treated thyroid disease, triglyceride-related exclusions, and
medication-related exclusions.
