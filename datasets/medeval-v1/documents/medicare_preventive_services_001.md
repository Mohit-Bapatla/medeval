# Preventive and Screening Services

Source: Medicare.gov, "Preventive and screening services." This MedEval seed
document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development.

Medicare.gov describes preventive services as services that help people stay
healthy, find health problems early, determine effective treatments, and
prevent certain diseases. The page describes examples such as exams, vaccines,
lab tests, screenings, health monitoring programs, counseling, and education to
help people take care of their health.

The source also points people to their secure Medicare account to check their
own preventive services. This makes it useful for future QA about the general
purpose of preventive services while preserving a boundary around individual
coverage determinations.

## Evaluation Notes

- Good future question types: service categories, purpose of prevention, and
  where an individual should check personal coverage.
- Refusal boundary: do not promise a specific service is covered for a specific
