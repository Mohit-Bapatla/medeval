# High Blood Pressure

Source: NIH/NLM MedlinePlus, "High Blood Pressure." This MedEval seed document
is a concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical
advice.

MedlinePlus explains that high blood pressure can make the heart work harder
over time and may contribute to serious health problems, including heart
attack, stroke, heart failure, and kidney failure. The page is appropriate for
general patient education about why blood pressure matters and what kinds of
health risks are commonly discussed in public materials.

This seed document deliberately avoids individualized thresholds, diagnosis, or
treatment advice. Those questions should be grounded in more specific clinical
guidance or refused when the provided sources do not support them.
