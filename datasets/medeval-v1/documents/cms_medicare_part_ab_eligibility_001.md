# Original Medicare Part A and Part B Eligibility and Enrollment

Source: CMS, "Original Medicare (Part A and B) Eligibility and Enrollment."
This MedEval seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation
development.

CMS explains eligibility and enrollment concepts for Original Medicare Part A
and Part B. The page describes premium-free Part A eligibility as tied to being
entitled to Medicare based on a worker's earnings record and a specified number
of quarters of coverage. For premium-free Part A based on age, CMS describes
age 65 or older and eligibility for monthly Social Security or Railroad
Retirement Board cash benefits as key conditions.

CMS also explains that some people receiving Social Security or Railroad
Retirement Board benefits before age 65 may be enrolled automatically, while
others may need to apply through Social Security.
