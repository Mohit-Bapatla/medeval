# Depression

Source: NIH/NLM MedlinePlus, "Depression." This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical advice.

MedlinePlus says major depression symptoms include a depressed mood or a loss of
interest. The source says those symptoms affect daily activities and last for at
least two weeks.

MedlinePlus describes persistent depressive disorder, also called dysthymia or
dysthymic disorder, as having less severe depressive symptoms that last longer,
usually for at least two years.

For diagnosis, the page says symptoms must occur most of the day, nearly every
day, for at least two weeks. One symptom must be a depressed mood or a loss of
interest in most activities. The page also notes that medical tests may be done
to rule out other medical conditions, and that certain medicines and medical
conditions may cause symptoms like depression.
