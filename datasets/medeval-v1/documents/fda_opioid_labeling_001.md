# FDA Opioid Pain Medicine Labeling Update

Source: FDA drug safety communication on opioid pain medicine labeling. This
MedEval seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation
development, not medication advice.

FDA states that it is requiring safety labeling changes for opioid pain
medicines to further emphasize risks associated with long-term use. The update
includes changes intended to avoid misinterpretation that evidence supports
opioid analgesic safety and effectiveness for an indefinitely long duration.
The communication also emphasizes that higher doses are associated with greater
risk of serious harm and that serious risks persist over the course of therapy.

FDA also describes labeling updates for extended-release and long-acting opioid
pain medicines, including that they should be used only when alternative
therapies are inadequate for severe and persistent pain.
