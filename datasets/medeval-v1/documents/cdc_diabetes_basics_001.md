# Diabetes Basics

Source: CDC, "Diabetes Basics." This MedEval seed document is a concise
attribution summary for evaluation development, not medical advice.

CDC describes diabetes as a chronic health condition that affects how the body
turns food into energy. The page distinguishes type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes,
and gestational diabetes. The source is useful for future QA about basic
definitions, broad condition categories, and when a question asks for a general
public-health explanation rather than individualized diagnosis or treatment.
