# What Is the National Diabetes Prevention Program?

Source: CDC, "What Is the National DPP?" This MedEval seed document is a
concise attribution summary for evaluation development.

The CDC describes the National Diabetes Prevention Program as a partnership of
public and private organizations that makes evidence-based lifestyle change
programs more available to people with prediabetes or other risk factors for
type 2 diabetes. The program is intended to help participants reduce their risk
of type 2 diabetes and improve overall health.

The source identifies several partner groups, including federal agencies, state
and local health departments, national and community organizations, employers,
public and private insurers, health care professionals, university community
education programs, and wellness-focused businesses.
