# Safe Use of Acetaminophen

Source: FDA, "Acetaminophen." This MedEval seed document is a concise
attribution summary for evaluation development, not medication advice.

FDA describes acetaminophen as a medicine used to temporarily relieve pain and
reduce fever. It may appear as the only active ingredient or be combined with
other active ingredients in prescription and over-the-counter products. FDA
warns that people should read product labels, know whether their medicines
contain acetaminophen, avoid using more than one acetaminophen-containing
product at a time, and follow dosing directions.

The source warns that taking too much acetaminophen can cause severe liver
injury. It also advises people with liver disease or substantial alcohol use to
ask a health care professional before use.
