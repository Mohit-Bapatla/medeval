# How Can I Find Out If I Am Eligible for Medicaid?

Source: Medicaid.gov, "How Can I Find Out If I'm Eligible for Medicaid?" This
MedEval seed document is a concise attribution summary for evaluation
development, not benefits advice.

Medicaid.gov says that to find out for sure whether someone is eligible for
Medicaid, the person must contact their state Medicaid agency.

The page tells users to choose their state to get the contact information they
need to get started. The source is useful for questions about where eligibility
confirmation must come from, but it does not itself decide a person's Medicaid
eligibility.
