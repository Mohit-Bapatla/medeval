# Synthetic Hospital Volunteer Program

This is a fictional demo document for MedEval development. It does not describe
a real hospital, real patients, real students, or a real volunteer program.

## Overview

Demo Valley Hospital offers a synthetic volunteer program for testing document
ingestion and retrieval workflows. Volunteers may support wayfinding, visitor
desk greeting, patient transport dispatch assistance, and supply cart restocking
under staff supervision.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 16 years old. Applicants under 18 must submit a
guardian consent form. The demo program asks for a weekly commitment of one
four-hour shift for eight weeks.

## Required Documents

Applicants should provide a photo ID, emergency contact form, immunization
attestation, and completed confidentiality agreement. This synthetic document
does not request or include patient information.

## Timeline

Demo applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. The fictional spring cohort
deadline is March 15. Orientation is listed as the first Saturday in April.
