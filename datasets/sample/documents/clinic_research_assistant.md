# Synthetic Clinic Research Assistant Opportunity

This is fictional demo content for MedEval. It is not connected to a real clinic,
study, student record, patient record, or hiring process.

## Role Summary

The Demo Community Clinic research assistant opportunity supports literature
review, spreadsheet quality checks, survey packet assembly, and meeting note
cleanup. The role is designed for retrieval testing around eligibility,
responsibilities, deadlines, and onboarding requirements.

## Eligibility

Applicants should have completed introductory biology or public health coursework
in this synthetic scenario. Prior research experience is helpful but not required.
Applicants must be at least 18 years old for this fictional opportunity.

## Schedule

The role asks for six hours per week for ten weeks. A fictional application
deadline is September 10. Selected demo applicants attend an online orientation
before receiving project materials.

## Boundaries

The assistant does not access real patient charts, protected health information,
or identifiable private data in this synthetic example.
