# Synthetic HIPAA Training Policy

This policy is fictional and simplified for MedEval retrieval development. It is
not legal advice, clinical guidance, or an official HIPAA compliance document.

## Purpose

The synthetic policy describes privacy training expectations for demo volunteers,
shadowing participants, and onboarding users. It helps test retrieval for
questions about confidentiality, badge access, and training completion.

## Training Completion

Participants must complete the fictional HIPAA training module before receiving
badge access. The module includes privacy principles, reporting suspected
privacy incidents, and avoiding use of identifiable patient details in notes.

## Attestation

After training, participants sign a demo confidentiality attestation. The
attestation confirms they understand the sample program's privacy expectations
and will not collect or share identifiable information.

## Escalation

Questions about privacy, lost paperwork, or accidental disclosure should be
escalated to the fictional program coordinator in this demo scenario.
