# Synthetic Volunteer Onboarding Requirements

This is demo-only onboarding content for MedEval. It does not include real
healthcare validation, real patient data, private student data, or real policy.

## Checklist

Before a synthetic volunteer begins, the onboarding packet requires:

- signed confidentiality acknowledgement
- HIPAA training module completion
- infection prevention overview
- badge request form
- emergency contact form
- supervisor assignment

## Timing

The fictional onboarding packet should be completed at least five business days
before the first scheduled shift. Badge requests are processed after the demo
supervisor approves the checklist.

## Training

HIPAA training covers privacy basics, minimum necessary access, reporting lost
paperwork, and avoiding discussion of identifiable information in public areas.
This is simplified demo text and is not official compliance guidance.
