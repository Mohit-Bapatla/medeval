# Synthetic Healthcare Shadowing Opportunity

This fictional document is provided only for MedEval sample retrieval tests. It
does not describe a real organization, clinician, student, or patient encounter.

## Opportunity

Demo Northside Health hosts a synthetic observation-only shadowing opportunity.
Participants observe front desk workflows, care team huddles, and simulated
patient education conversations created for training.

## Requirements

Participants must be at least 17 years old, submit a guardian consent form if
under 18, complete a confidentiality acknowledgement, and wear a visitor badge.
Closed-toe shoes are required in the fictional clinic areas.

## Application Materials

The demo application asks for a short statement of interest, availability for
weekday mornings, and confirmation that the participant understands the
observation-only scope.

## Limitations

Participants may not provide care, document in records, photograph materials, or
ask simulated patients for identifying information.
